# Decisions

## In force

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2026-11-19 | Open cup clamped under the switch's own nut, not a closed cap. A cap pressed onto the thin nut would be knocked off by the same jackets, tape needs a flat area the handset face does not have beside the switch, and a closed cap cannot have its nut tightened. The cup needs no glue and comes off again. |
| 2026-11-19 | The lever stays usable with a pen. Fully locking it would need a second part (a lid); add one only if the cup alone is not enough. |
| 2026-11-19 | The cup carries a hex well for the nut and is turned by hand, instead of a round cup tightened with a nut driver. That drops the tool, the tool measurement and the wider bore it needed. Cost: a jacket could in principle turn the cup loose; if that happens, snug it again or add a drop of removable threadlocker. |
| 2026-11-19 | No edge-distance measurement or check: the cup is under 2 mm wider per side than the nut corners unless the lever swing forces it wider, and overhanging the face edge slightly would not stop it working. Look at it when fitting. |
