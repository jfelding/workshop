## 2026-11-27 Single case cut from oralbox2

Source: DrJones's oralbox2.stl and oralbox2-widetol.3mf from Printables, CC BY 4.0 (vendor/oralbox2/).
Read from the mesh: the halves lie open with the hinge along X at y = 0; the clip half has two clips centred at
y = −10.0 and −25.0 (lane pitch 15.0), the other half two pegs (footprint y 12.5–22.0, 16.5 mm tall) and vent
slots y 6.5–28.5. Both rims at z = 9.5; folding the original about that line overlaps 2.53 mm³ (knuckles only),
the smallest of a scan over z 4–12.
Built stepped: full width to a copied end wall at x 26–28, strips y −31.5…−16.5 and 10…25 removed after it.
Result 100.0 × 69.0 × 16.0 mm open, 14.5 cm³ (original 18.7), each half one watertight solid; folded overlap
2.53 mm³, unchanged. Widetol identical except the hinge. Mesh lesson: a cut placed exactly on an existing face
(the clip's end at x 26.5) left a zero-volume sliver in the union; cuts now sit 0.5 mm off any face.
